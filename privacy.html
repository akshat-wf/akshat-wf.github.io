Privacy policy

WealthFlow, last updated 5 October 2026

WealthFlow is a personal finance app used privately by one household. It has no server, no accounts of its own, no advertising and no analytics. The developer never receives your data.

What the app reads

  • SMS messages on the phone, to find bank and card transaction alerts.
  • Gmail, read-only (gmail.readonly), to find bank alerts and card statements. The app never sends, changes or deletes email.
  • Google Drive (drive), only for the optional spouse sync described below.

Where your data is kept

Everything the app reads or works out stays in the app's storage on your phone. It is sent only to the household's other phone, by one of these routes:

  • Directly between the two phones on the same Wi-Fi, encrypted.
  • Google Drive sync (optional): each phone writes encrypted change files into a Drive folder the household chose and shares between its own two Google accounts, and reads the other phone's files from it. The files are encrypted on the phone with a key derived from the household's passphrase, so Google cannot read them. The app only reads, writes and deletes its own sync files, named wfsync-…; it does not read anything else in your Drive.

Backups are encrypted with the household's passphrase and go only wherever you save or share them.

Google user data

WealthFlow's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide the features above, is not transferred to anyone else, and is not used for advertising.

Removing your data

Turning off a connection in the app stops further access. Revoke the app's Google access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app deletes its data from the phone; sync files can be deleted from the shared Drive folder.
